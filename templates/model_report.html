Model Selection Rationale
Why not just use Accuracy?

In cybersecurity threat detection, accuracy alone is misleading. A model that labels everything as "safe" on a mostly-safe dataset achieves high accuracy but is useless. We care more about Recall — catching as many real threats as possible — even at the cost of some false alarms.

Composite Selection Score
0.45 × F1-Score
0.30 × ROC-AUC
0.25 × Recall
